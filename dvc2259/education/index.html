51% of females aged 30 to 39 years had a higher education qualification

A higher percentage of younger female adults had a higher education qualification than males. Around half of females (51%) aged in their 30s held a higher education qualification, compared to 43% of males in the same age group.

This supports previously published data which showed that since the mid-1990s, women have outnumbered men on both undergraduate and postgraduate courses, and this gap between the sexes has continued to widen.

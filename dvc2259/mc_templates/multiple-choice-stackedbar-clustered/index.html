Question:

38% of women are educated to at least undergraduate degree level (or equilavent). What proportion of men are educated to the same level?

✕

Bad luck!

37% of men are educated to degree level or equivalent.

✓

Correct!

37% of men are educated to degree level or equivalent.

Younger female adults (aged between 20 and 59 years) had a higher percentage of “Level 4 or above” qualification compared with males. The biggest difference was seen in females and males aged in their 20’s and 30’s (44% and 36% for people aged 20 to 29 years, and 51% and 43% for people aged 30 to 39 years).

This supports previously published data which showed that since the mid-1990s, women have outnumbered men on both undergraduate and postgraduate courses, and this gap between the sexes continues to widen.

This trend shifts, however, when we look at older adults aged 60 and above, with males having a higher percentage of a “Level 4 or above” qualification compared with females. The biggest difference here was seen in adults aged 90 years and over, 21% of males compared with 11% of females.

In every age category, there was a higher percentage of males working as managers, directors, and senior officials than females.

Males had a higher percentage than females working as managers, directors, and senior officials

Age and sex, by occupation, of those in employment, England and Wales, 2021

Although males had a higher percentage working as managers, directors, and senior officials than females, females in most age groups had a higher percentage than males working in professional occupations – including scientists, engineers, and health professionals.

Males also hugely outnumbered females in skilled trades: jobs like farmers, mechanics, electricians, and chefs, whereas more females than males worked in sectors like care and leisure, as well as in administrative and secretarial jobs.

Males had a higher percentage than females rating their health as good or very good

For most age groups, males rated their health higher than females. The biggest differences can be seen for males and females in their late teens (16 to 19 years, 71% for males compared with 66% for females), and in their 20s (20 to 29 years, 62% for males compared with 57% for female).

On average males generally rated their health higher than females

Age and sex, by good and very good health, England and Wales, 2021

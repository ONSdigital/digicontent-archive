Question:

For which of these age groups was the gap between the proportion of males and females in employment the largest?

Correct!

The gap between males and females in employment is widest between the ages of 30 to 39 years

There were more males than females in employment for all age groups, with the exception of people aged 16 to 19 years. The widest percentage difference between males and females of working age was for people in their 30s (86% of males compared with 75% of females). This could possibly be explained by females in this age group having a higher percentage looking after the family or home than males (13% compared with 2%).

Men also generally stop working later than women. The percentage difference between males and females for people in their 30s was equally as wide for people in their 60s (47% of males compared with 36% of females). Similarly, the percentage of males aged 70 to 79 years was two times higher than females in the same age category (10% compared with 5%).

On average a higher percentage of males were in employment than females

Question:

At what age do more than half (59%) of people live in a home that is owned outright (with no outstanding mortgage)?

✕

Bad luck!

59% of adults aged 60 to 69 years live in a home that is owned outright

✓

Correct!

59% of adults aged 60 to 69 years live in a home that is owned outright

Over half of adults in their 60s (59%) lived in a property that was owned outright and this increases to three-quarters for adults in their 70s and 80s (75% and 76%). Just under 1 in 10 (9%) of adults in their 30s lived in a property which was owned outright.

This could be partly explained by additional Census 2021 data which has shown that almost 1 in 8 (12%) adults aged 30 to 34 years were living with their parents in Census 2021, up from 9% a decade earlier.

Around three-quarters of older adults lived in a home that was owned outright

Age by housing tenure, England and Wales, 2021

Notes:
1. Totals may not add to 100% as percentages have been calculated out of the overall population for each age group using rounded data.
2. This analysis focuses on usual residents (people within households) as age is individual. However, data on housing tenure is only available at the household level, so tenure information would be applied to all individuals within the household.

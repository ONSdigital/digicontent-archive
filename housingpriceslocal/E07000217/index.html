Housing prices in Woking

How average house prices and rents are changing in Woking.
Last updated:
19 August 2026

In Woking:

!
Warning:

Local housing data are based on a smaller number of properties than our national estimates, meaning short-term trends are more variable. We advise looking at trends over a year or longer.

We regularly revise our house prices estimates. UK-level rent estimates for the latest two months, and all house price data, are provisional and subject to revision.

Figures in this article are not seasonally adjusted.

You can explore data on housing affordability with our tool: "How affordable are homes in your neighbourhood?".

Our small area housing affordability measures use a different way of calculating house prices to allow us to compare housing costs with incomes. This means affordability house prices look different to those on this page.

Average house price in Woking was £434,000 in June 2026

The provisional average house price in Woking in June 2026 was £434,000. This was lower than the average of £446,000 in June 2025 (revised), a 2.7% fall.

Across the South East, the average house price in June 2026 was £380,000, which was in line with a year earlier (£379,000).

Across the UK, a home sold for an average of £272,000 in June 2026, which was up slightly from the June 2025 average of £267,000.

In the year to June 2026, the average price for terraced properties in Woking fell by 1.4%, while the average price for detached properties decreased by 3.9%.

For each property type, average prices as of June 2026 in Woking were:

  • Detached properties: £914,000
  • Semi-detached properties: £477,000
  • Terraced properties: £377,000
  • Flats and maisonettes: £251,000

Average private rents fell slightly in Woking

The average monthly private rent in Woking was £1,619 in July 2026. This was a slight decrease from £1,647 in July 2025, a 1.7% fall.

Across the South East, the average monthly rent was £1,419, up from £1,379 a year earlier.

Across the UK, the average rent price in July 2026 was £1,393, which was up from £1,343 in July 2025.

In Woking, the average rent for terraced properties fell by 1.3%, while for detached properties, it decreased by 2.3%.

Average rent for three bed properties fell by 1.5%, while the average for four-or-more bed properties decreased by 2.7%.

By how many bedrooms there are in a property, average rents as of July 2026 in Woking were:

  • One bedroom: £1,132
  • Two bedrooms: £1,442
  • Three bedrooms: £1,759
  • Four or more bedrooms: £2,691

Taking the data instead by property type, average rents were:

  • Flats and maisonettes: £1,291
  • Terraced properties: £1,490
  • Semi-detached properties: £1,760
  • Detached properties: £2,506

First-time buyers in Woking paid £330,000 on average in June 2026

The provisional average price paid by first-time buyers was £330,000 in June 2026. This was lower than the revised June 2025 average of £338,000.

In comparison, home-movers in Woking paid an average of £573,000 in June 2026. This was lower than £591,000 a year earlier.

In June 2026, the average price was £475,000 in the South East and £335,000 in Great Britain.

We do not produce figures for first-time buyer prices by type of property. Average house prices by property type for price paid data are available above.

Average house price for mortgage buyers fell in Woking

The average price of a home bought with a mortgage in Woking was £435,000 in June 2026 (provisional). This was lower than an average of £446,000 in June 2025 (revised).

Across the South East, the average price of a home bought with a mortgage was £384,000, in line with £382,000 a year earlier.

Across Great Britain, the average price for buying with a mortage in June 2026 was £281,000, which was up slightly from an average of £276,000 in June 2025.

Our mortgage calculator shows how monthly repayments are changing at local area level, based on the latest house prices and interest rates.

For cash buyers in Woking, the average was £431,000 in June 2026 (provisional), which was down from a revised figure of £444,000 a year earlier.

In June 2026, the average was £370,000 in the South East and £258,000 in Great Britain.

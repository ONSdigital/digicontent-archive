Housing prices in Glasgow

How average house prices and rents are changing in Glasgow.
Last updated:
16 September 2026

In Glasgow:

!
Warning:

Local housing data are based on a smaller number of properties than our national estimates, meaning short-term trends are more variable. We advise looking at trends over a year or longer.

We regularly revise our house prices estimates. All of the house price data from the last 12 months and UK-level rent estimates for the latest two months are provisional and subject to revision.

Figures in this article are not seasonally adjusted.

Scotland rents data are mainly for advertised new lets, which were not subject to Scotland's in-tenancy price increase cap and are not subject to temporary changes to the Rent Adjudication system. See the More data on the housing market section for further details on Scotland's rents data.

Average house price in Glasgow was £194,000 in July 2026

The provisional average house price in Glasgow in July 2026 was £194,000. This was higher than the average of £189,000 in July 2025 (revised), a 2.6% rise.

Across Scotland, the average house price in July 2026 was £196,000, which was more than a year earlier (£192,000).

Across the UK, a home sold for an average of £273,000 in July 2026, which was up slightly from the July 2025 average of £269,000.

In the year to July 2026, the average price for semi-detached properties in Glasgow rose by 5.4%, while the average price for flats increased by 1.7%.

For each property type, average prices as of July 2026 in Glasgow were:

  • Detached properties: £502,000
  • Semi-detached properties: £305,000
  • Terraced properties: £249,000
  • Flats and maisonettes: £163,000

Average private rents rose slightly in Greater Glasgow

Glasgow is in the broad rental market area of Greater Glasgow.

Private rents in Greater Glasgow averaged at £1,266 a month in August 2026. This was a slight increase from £1,251 in August 2025, a 1.3% rise.

Across Scotland, the average monthly rent was £1,013, up slightly from £1,002 a year earlier.

Across the UK, the average rent price in August 2026 was £1,400, which was up from £1,348 in August 2025.

In Greater Glasgow, average rents on flats or maisonettes rose by 2.1%, while for detached properties, the average stayed around the same.

For one bed properties, average rents rose by 2.9%, while the average for four-or-more bed properties increased by 2.0%.

By how many bedrooms there are in a property, average rents as of August 2026 in Greater Glasgow were:

  • One bedroom: £843
  • Two bedrooms: £1,089
  • Three bedrooms: £1,340
  • Four or more bedrooms: £2,280

Taking the data instead by property type, average rents were:

  • Flats and maisonettes: £1,008
  • Terraced properties: £1,189
  • Semi-detached properties: £1,310
  • Detached properties: £2,109

First-time buyers in Glasgow paid £174,000 on average in July 2026

The provisional average price paid by first-time buyers was £174,000 in July 2026. This was higher than the revised July 2025 average of £170,000.

In comparison, home-movers in Glasgow paid an average of £242,000 in July 2026. This was higher than £235,000 a year earlier.

In July 2026, the average price was £247,000 in Scotland and £336,000 in Great Britain.

We do not produce figures for first-time buyer prices by type of property. Average house prices by property type for price paid data are available above.

Average house price for mortgage buyers rose in Glasgow

The average price of a home bought with a mortgage in Glasgow was £199,000 in July 2026 (provisional). This was higher than an average of £194,000 in July 2025 (revised).

Across Scotland, the average price of a home bought with a mortgage was £204,000, up from £200,000 a year earlier.

Across Great Britain, the average price for buying with a mortage in July 2026 was £282,000, which was up slightly from an average of £278,000 in July 2025.

Our mortgage calculator shows how monthly repayments are changing at local area level, based on the latest house prices and interest rates.

For cash buyers in Glasgow, the average was £181,000 in July 2026 (provisional), which was up from a revised figure of £177,000 a year earlier.

In July 2026, the average was £181,000 in Scotland and £259,000 in Great Britain.

More data on the housing market

This article is regularly updated with the most recent rents and house prices information. The full time series for rental prices can be found in our associated data releases. The full time series for house prices can be found in the UK House Price Index reports on gov.uk.

You can also read our most recent Private rent and house prices bulletin.

The methods used to produce the rental prices can be found in the Price Index of Private Rents Quality and Methodology Information. The methods used to produce the house prices can be found in the House Price Index Quality and Methodology Information.

Because of data collection limitations, Scotland rents data (underlying the PIPR's stock measure) are mainly for advertised new lets, which were not subject to Scotland's in-tenancy price increase cap and are not subject to temporary changes to the Rent Adjudication system. We advise users to bear this in mind when interpreting estimates for Scotland and comparing with other UK countries (see Section 9: Measuring the data).

Housing prices in Rhondda Cynon Taf

How average house prices and rents are changing in Rhondda Cynon Taf.
Last updated:
16 September 2026

In Rhondda Cynon Taf:

!
Warning:

Local housing data are based on a smaller number of properties than our national estimates, meaning short-term trends are more variable. We advise looking at trends over a year or longer.

We regularly revise our house prices estimates. UK-level rent estimates for the latest two months, and all house price data, are provisional and subject to revision.

Figures in this article are not seasonally adjusted.

You can explore data on housing affordability with our tool: "How affordable are homes in your neighbourhood?".

Our small area housing affordability measures use a different way of calculating house prices to allow us to compare housing costs with incomes. This means affordability house prices look different to those on this page.

Average house price in Rhondda Cynon Taf was £164,000 in July 2026

The provisional average house price in Rhondda Cynon Taf in July 2026 was £164,000. This was higher than the average of £157,000 in July 2025 (revised), a 4.7% rise.

Across Wales, the average house price in July 2026 was £215,000, which was more than a year earlier (£210,000).

Across the UK, a home sold for an average of £273,000 in July 2026, which was up slightly from the July 2025 average of £269,000.

In the year to July 2026, the average price for terraced properties in Rhondda Cynon Taf rose by 5.0%, while the average price for flats stayed around the same over the year.

For each property type, average prices as of July 2026 in Rhondda Cynon Taf were:

  • Detached properties: £287,000
  • Semi-detached properties: £179,000
  • Terraced properties: £143,000
  • Flats and maisonettes: £86,000

Average private rents rose slightly in Rhondda Cynon Taf

The average monthly private rent in Rhondda Cynon Taf was £754 in August 2026. This was a slight increase from £741 in August 2025, a 1.7% rise.

Across Wales, the average monthly rent was £846, up from £811 a year earlier.

Across the UK, the average rent price in August 2026 was £1,400, which was up from £1,348 in August 2025.

In Rhondda Cynon Taf, the average rent for terraced properties rose by 2.2%, while for flats or maisonettes, it increased by 1.4%.

Average rent for three bed properties rose by 2.1%, while the average for four-or-more bed properties stayed around the same.

By how many bedrooms there are in a property, average rents as of August 2026 in Rhondda Cynon Taf were:

  • One bedroom: £528
  • Two bedrooms: £675
  • Three bedrooms: £758
  • Four or more bedrooms: £995

Taking the data instead by property type, average rents were:

  • Flats and maisonettes: £588
  • Terraced properties: £733
  • Semi-detached properties: £802
  • Detached properties: £967

First-time buyers in Rhondda Cynon Taf paid £152,000 on average in July 2026

The provisional average price paid by first-time buyers was £152,000 in July 2026. This was higher than the revised July 2025 average of £145,000.

In comparison, home-movers in Rhondda Cynon Taf paid an average of £188,000 in July 2026. This was higher than £180,000 a year earlier.

In July 2026, the average price was £257,000 in Wales and £336,000 in Great Britain.

We do not produce figures for first-time buyer prices by type of property. Average house prices by property type for price paid data are available above.

Average house price for mortgage buyers rose in Rhondda Cynon Taf

The average price of a home bought with a mortgage in Rhondda Cynon Taf was £167,000 in July 2026 (provisional). This was higher than an average of £160,000 in July 2025 (revised).

Across Wales, the average price of a home bought with a mortgage was £215,000, up from £210,000 a year earlier.

Across Great Britain, the average price for buying with a mortage in July 2026 was £282,000, which was up slightly from an average of £278,000 in July 2025.

Our mortgage calculator shows how monthly repayments are changing at local area level, based on the latest house prices and interest rates.

For cash buyers in Rhondda Cynon Taf, the average was £156,000 in July 2026 (provisional), which was up from a revised figure of £149,000 a year earlier.

In July 2026, the average was £214,000 in Wales and £259,000 in Great Britain.

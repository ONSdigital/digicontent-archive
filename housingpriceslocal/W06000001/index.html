Housing prices on the Isle of Anglesey

How average house prices and rents are changing on the Isle of Anglesey.
Last updated:
19 August 2026

On the Isle of Anglesey:

!
Warning:

Local housing data are based on a smaller number of properties than our national estimates, meaning short-term trends are more variable. We advise looking at trends over a year or longer.

We regularly revise our house prices estimates. UK-level rent estimates for the latest two months, and all house price data, are provisional and subject to revision.

Figures in this article are not seasonally adjusted.

You can explore data on housing affordability with our tool: "How affordable are homes in your neighbourhood?".

Our small area housing affordability measures use a different way of calculating house prices to allow us to compare housing costs with incomes. This means affordability house prices look different to those on this page.

Average house price on the Isle of Anglesey was £240,000 in June 2026

The provisional average house price on the Isle of Anglesey in June 2026 was £240,000. This was higher than the average of £225,000 in June 2025 (revised), a 6.7% rise.

Across Wales, the average house price in June 2026 was £213,000, which was slightly more than a year earlier (£209,000).

This meant that, in June 2026, the Isle of Anglesey had the fourth highest average house price in Wales.

Across the UK, a home sold for an average of £272,000 in June 2026, which was up slightly from the June 2025 average of £267,000.

In the year to June 2026, the average price for terraced properties on the Isle of Anglesey rose by 7.7%, while the average price for flats increased by 3.5%.

For each property type, average prices as of June 2026 on the Isle of Anglesey were:

  • Detached properties: £329,000
  • Semi-detached properties: £212,000
  • Terraced properties: £168,000
  • Flats and maisonettes: £143,000

Average private rents rose on the Isle of Anglesey

The average monthly private rent on the Isle of Anglesey was £710 in July 2026. This was an increase from £682 in July 2025, a 4.2% rise.

Across Wales, the average monthly rent was £843, up from £807 a year earlier.

Across the UK, the average rent price in July 2026 was £1,393, which was up from £1,343 in July 2025.

On the Isle of Anglesey, the average rent for terraced properties rose by 4.7%, while for flats or maisonettes, it increased by 3.7%.

Average rent for three bed properties rose by 4.4%, while the average for four-or-more bed properties increased by 3.3%.

By how many bedrooms there are in a property, average rents as of July 2026 on the Isle of Anglesey were:

  • One bedroom: £547
  • Two bedrooms: £690
  • Three bedrooms: £793
  • Four or more bedrooms: £1,080

Taking the data instead by property type, average rents were:

  • Flats and maisonettes: £614
  • Terraced properties: £704
  • Semi-detached properties: £738
  • Detached properties: £874

First-time buyers on the Isle of Anglesey paid £197,000 on average in June 2026

The provisional average price paid by first-time buyers was £197,000 in June 2026. This was higher than the revised June 2025 average of £184,000.

In comparison, home-movers on the Isle of Anglesey paid an average of £282,000 in June 2026. This was higher than £265,000 a year earlier.

In June 2026, the average price was £255,000 in Wales and £335,000 in Great Britain.

We do not produce figures for first-time buyer prices by type of property. Average house prices by property type for price paid data are available above.

Average house price for mortgage buyers rose on the Isle of Anglesey

The average price of a home bought with a mortgage on the Isle of Anglesey was £228,000 in June 2026 (provisional). This was higher than an average of £213,000 in June 2025 (revised).

Across Wales, the average price of a home bought with a mortgage was £214,000, up slightly from £209,000 a year earlier.

Across Great Britain, the average price for buying with a mortage in June 2026 was £281,000, which was up slightly from an average of £276,000 in June 2025.

Our mortgage calculator shows how monthly repayments are changing at local area level, based on the latest house prices and interest rates.

For cash buyers on the Isle of Anglesey, the average was £254,000 in June 2026 (provisional), which was up from a revised figure of £238,000 a year earlier.

In June 2026, the average was £212,000 in Wales and £258,000 in Great Britain.

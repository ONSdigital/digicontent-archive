Housing prices in Causeway Coast and Glens

How average house prices and rents are changing in Causeway Coast and Glens.
Last updated:
19 August 2026

In Causeway Coast and Glens:

!
Warning:

Local housing data are based on a smaller number of properties than our national estimates, meaning short-term trends are more variable. We advise looking at trends over a year or longer.

We regularly revise our house prices estimates. UK-level rent estimates for the latest two months, and all house price data, are provisional and subject to revision.

Figures in this article are not seasonally adjusted.

Northern Ireland rents data are entirely for advertised new lets. This is a different method to other UK nations. See the More data on the housing market section for further details on comparability.

Average house price in Causeway Coast and Glens was £219,000 in April 2026 to June 2026

The provisional average house price in Causeway Coast and Glens in April 2026 to June 2026 was £219,000. This was higher than the average of £200,000 in April 2025 to June 2025 (revised), a 9.6% rise.

Across Northern Ireland, the average house price in April 2026 to June 2026 was £202,000, which was more than a year earlier (£185,000).

This meant that, in April 2026 to June 2026, Causeway Coast and Glens had the fourth highest average house price in Northern Ireland.

Across the UK, a home sold for an average of £272,000 in June 2026, which was up slightly from the June 2025 average of £267,000.

Average private rents rose in North Northern Ireland

The local authority of Causeway Coast and Glens overlaps with the broad rental market areas of North Northern Ireland, North West Northern Ireland and Lough Neagh Upper.

Average private rental price in North Northern Ireland rental market area, May 2026

Area boundaries are approximate

The average private rent for each of those areas in May 2026 was

  • North Northern Ireland: £811
  • North West Northern Ireland: £795
  • Lough Neagh Upper: £797

The rental area that is the best match for your selected local authority is North Northern Ireland. See the More data on the housing market section for details of how we matched local authority and rental areas.

Private rents in North Northern Ireland averaged at £811 a month in May 2026. This was an increase from £778 in May 2025, a 4.2% rise.

Across Northern Ireland, the average monthly rent was £875, up from £855 a year earlier.

Across the UK, the average rent price in May 2026 was £1,383, which was up from £1,339 in May 2025.

By how many bedrooms there are in a property, average rents as of May 2026 in North Northern Ireland rental area were:

  • One bedroom: £565
  • Two bedrooms: £708
  • Three bedrooms: £837
  • Four or more bedrooms: £1,107

Taking the data instead by property type, average rents were:

  • Flats and maisonettes: £708
  • Terraced properties: £774
  • Semi-detached properties: £855
  • Detached properties: £962

More data on the housing market

This article is regularly updated with the most recent rents and house prices information. The full time series for rental prices can be found in our associated data releases. The full time series for house prices can be found in the UK House Price Index reports on gov.uk.

You can also read our most recent Private rent and house prices bulletin.

The methods used to produce the rental prices can be found in the Price Index of Private Rents Quality and Methodology Information. The methods used to produce the house prices can be found in the House Price Index Quality and Methodology Information.

Northern Ireland rents data are provided for broad rental market areas, which cover a set of postcodes. Some local government districts in Northern Ireland overlap with several of these rental areas.

To get a best match of local authority to rental area, we looked at the proportion of postcodes per local authority that fell within each rental area. We exclude marginal matches, for clarity (where fewer than 1% of postcodes overlap).

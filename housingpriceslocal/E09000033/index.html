Housing prices in Westminster

How average house prices and rents are changing in Westminster.
Last updated:
19 August 2026

In Westminster:

!
Warning:

Local housing data are based on a smaller number of properties than our national estimates, meaning short-term trends are more variable. We advise looking at trends over a year or longer.

We regularly revise our house prices estimates. UK-level rent estimates for the latest two months, and all house price data, are provisional and subject to revision.

Figures in this article are not seasonally adjusted.

You can explore data on housing affordability with our tool: "How affordable are homes in your neighbourhood?".

Our small area housing affordability measures use a different way of calculating house prices to allow us to compare housing costs with incomes. This means affordability house prices look different to those on this page.

Average house price in Westminster was £854,000 in June 2026

The provisional average house price in Westminster in June 2026 was £854,000. This was lower than the average of £1,145,000 in June 2025 (revised), a 25.4% fall.

Across London, the average house price in June 2026 was £554,000, which was less than a year earlier (£568,000).

This meant that, in June 2026, Westminster had the second highest average house price in London.

Across the UK, a home sold for an average of £272,000 in June 2026, which was up slightly from the June 2025 average of £267,000.

In the year to June 2026, the average price for terraced properties in Westminster fell by 23.4%, while the average price for flats decreased by 25.7%.

For each property type, average prices as of June 2026 in Westminster were:

  • Detached properties: £3,687,000
  • Semi-detached properties: £2,581,000
  • Terraced properties: £1,548,000
  • Flats and maisonettes: £758,000

Average private rents fell slightly in Westminster

The average monthly private rent in Westminster was £3,179 in July 2026. This was a slight decrease from £3,244 in July 2025, a 2.0% fall.

Across London, the average monthly rent was £2,317, up from £2,250 a year earlier.

Across the UK, the average rent price in July 2026 was £1,393, which was up from £1,343 in July 2025.

In Westminster, the average rent for flats or maisonettes fell by 1.9%, while for terraced properties, it decreased by 2.1%.

Average rent for one bed properties fell by 1.5%, while the average for four-or-more bed properties decreased by 2.8%.

By how many bedrooms there are in a property, average rents as of July 2026 in Westminster were:

  • One bedroom: £2,533
  • Two bedrooms: £3,286
  • Three bedrooms: £3,872
  • Four or more bedrooms: £5,396

Taking the data instead by property type, average rents were:

  • Flats and maisonettes: £2,941
  • Terraced properties: £3,789
  • Semi-detached properties: £2,905
  • Detached properties: £2,893

First-time buyers in Westminster paid £766,000 on average in June 2026

The provisional average price paid by first-time buyers was £766,000 in June 2026. This was lower than the revised June 2025 average of £1,025,000.

In comparison, home-movers in Westminster paid an average of £1,014,000 in June 2026. This was lower than £1,356,000 a year earlier.

In June 2026, the average price was £697,000 in London and £335,000 in Great Britain.

We do not produce figures for first-time buyer prices by type of property. Average house prices by property type for price paid data are available above.

Average house price for mortgage buyers fell in Westminster

The average price of a home bought with a mortgage in Westminster was £837,000 in June 2026 (provisional). This was lower than an average of £1,119,000 in June 2025 (revised).

Across London, the average price of a home bought with a mortgage was £547,000, down from £558,000 a year earlier.

Across Great Britain, the average price for buying with a mortage in June 2026 was £281,000, which was up slightly from an average of £276,000 in June 2025.

Our mortgage calculator shows how monthly repayments are changing at local area level, based on the latest house prices and interest rates.

For cash buyers in Westminster, the average was £882,000 in June 2026 (provisional), which was down from a revised figure of £1,185,000 a year earlier.

In June 2026, the average was £589,000 in London and £258,000 in Great Britain.

About this article

The content in this article is generated using semi-automated journalism, based on rules pre-programmed by Office for National Statistics (ONS) staff.

Articles are not available for Isles of Scilly and City of London, as the survey samples of the resident population are too small.

The percentage of people testing positive for coronavirus (COVID-19) increased in England, and the trends were uncertain in Wales and Scotland in the week ending 13 March 2023.

Official reported estimates of the rate of COVID-19 infections in private households in England, Wales and Scotland.

Modelled estimates are used to calculate the official reported estimate. The model smooths the series to understand the trend and is revised each week to incorporate new test results.

Estimates with 95% credible intervals
